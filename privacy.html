privacy policy

last updated: 10 april 2026

plain language, no legalese. your data is yours. we use it to make liv work for you — nothing else. this policy covers how liv handles data from connected services, including Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Contacts), and describes our commitments under Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

who we are

liv is built by Leonardo Borges Barbosa, an Australian sole trader (ABN 52 893 209 032) based in Zetland, NSW 2017, Australia, reachable at support@agent.liv4all.com. when we say "we", "us", or "liv4all" in this policy, that's who we mean. we are the data controller for the personal data described below and operate under the Australian Privacy Principles set out in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

this policy applies to the liv4all service operated at the following URLs:

  • product dashboard — https://app.liv4all.com/ (where you sign in, connect Google Workspace, and manage your agent)
  • marketing site — https://hello.liv4all.com/ (this site)
  • support — support@agent.liv4all.com

what we collect

we only collect data that's necessary to provide your personal AI chief of staff:

  • account info — your name, email address, and phone number (for messaging channels like WhatsApp or Signal).
  • conversation history — messages between you and your liv agent, stored to maintain context and memory across sessions.
  • connected-service OAuth tokens — refresh and access tokens for services you connect (Google Workspace, etc.). we store tokens, never your passwords. tokens are encrypted at rest and handled exclusively by our secrets-aware proxy (see "security architecture" below).
  • Google Workspace data — when you authorise Gmail, Calendar, or Contacts access, liv fetches the specific data needed to complete tasks you've asked for. see "Google Workspace data & scopes" below for the full breakdown.
  • agent workspace files — notes, preferences, and memory files your agent creates to serve you better.

what we don't collect

  • we don't track you across the web.
  • we don't use cookies or analytics on this site.
  • we don't collect data from people you interact with through liv beyond what they've sent to you (e.g. the content of an email they sent you that liv processes on your behalf).
  • we don't store your passwords for any connected service.
  • we don't sell or rent your data to anyone, ever.

Google Workspace data & scopes

liv requests access to Google Workspace via OAuth. you see and approve every scope on Google's consent screen before any access is granted, and you can revoke access at any time. the table below lists every Google scope liv requests, the Google API it corresponds to, what data it exposes to liv, and the user-facing feature it enables.

scope data accessed user-facing feature
calendar events, availability, attendees scheduling, reminders, creating/updating invites
contacts.readonly contact names, emails, phone numbers finding the right person when you ask liv to email or schedule with them
gmail.readonly inbox messages, threads, labels, headers, bodies, attachments metadata reading, summarising, and searching your inbox so your agent can answer questions like "what did legal say about the contract?" and keep context on ongoing threads

what liv will never do with Gmail access: liv does not request gmail.delete or any scope that would permanently delete messages. liv will never delete email from your account — archive and label only. liv does not scan your mailbox for advertising, marketing, data mining, or any purpose other than executing the task you asked it to perform.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use

liv4all's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

specifically, liv commits that:

  • user-facing features only. we only access your Google user data to provide or improve the user-facing features of liv that you have asked for (reading your inbox, drafting replies, scheduling meetings, etc.). we do not use Google user data for any unrelated purpose.
  • no transfer to others except in the narrow circumstances allowed by the policy: (i) as necessary to provide or improve those user-facing features, (ii) to comply with applicable law, (iii) as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with notice to users, or (iv) with your explicit consent.
  • no advertising. we do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeted, personalised, or interest-based ads.
  • no humans reading data except (i) with your explicit consent (e.g. you share a debug bundle with us when reporting an issue), (ii) when necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, (iii) to comply with applicable law, or (iv) when the data is aggregated and used for internal operations in accordance with applicable privacy laws. these exceptions are narrow and invoked rarely — liv is built so that, in normal operation, no human at liv4all ever reads your Gmail data.
  • no training of generalised AI/ML models. we do not use your Google Workspace data — including Gmail content, Calendar events, or Contacts — to develop, improve, or train generalised or non-personalised AI/ML models. the large-language-model providers liv uses for agent reasoning are contractually bound not to train on data sent via their APIs (see "subprocessors" below).

how your Gmail content is processed

when liv needs to act on your mailbox — for example, to summarise a thread or draft a reply — the flow is:

  • liv fetches only the specific messages needed for the task you asked for, directly from the Gmail API using your stored OAuth token.
  • the fetched content is passed to a large-language-model provider (see "subprocessors") so your agent can reason about it. these providers operate under zero-retention / no-training API terms.
  • fetched Gmail content may be held in a short-term cache (maximum 24 hours) so your agent can answer follow-up questions about the same thread without re-fetching. after 24 hours the cached content is automatically purged.
  • durable agent memory stores summaries and references (e.g. "replied to Jane about the Q2 report on 8 April") rather than full message bodies, unless you explicitly ask liv to remember a specific message verbatim.
  • nothing from your Gmail account is ever used to train AI models, shown to advertisers, or shared outside the subprocessors listed below.

security architecture — the secrets-aware proxy

a core security control in liv is a TLS-terminating egress proxy that sits between the agent runtime and every outbound network call. the proxy performs man-in-the-middle TLS termination for traffic originating inside the agent sandbox, then:

  • injects secrets on the way out. when the agent makes a request that needs an OAuth token or API key, the proxy rewrites the request headers to attach the correct secret. the agent itself — and any LLM reasoning over its context — never sees the raw token.
  • scrubs secrets on the way in. responses are scanned and any matching secret material is redacted before being returned to the agent's context window.
  • enforces an egress allowlist. agents can only reach the specific APIs they need (Google, messaging providers, the configured LLM endpoints). arbitrary outbound traffic is blocked.

the practical effect: your Google OAuth tokens are never transmitted to any LLM provider and are never present in any data that leaves our infrastructure. tokens live only in the proxy's secrets store and are injected at the moment of each Google API call.

subprocessors

liv uses a small, named set of subprocessors to deliver the service. each one is bound by contract to process data only on our instructions and not to train models on it.

subprocessor purpose data shared
Amazon Web Services (AWS), ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) hosting, compute, storage, encryption-at-rest all user data at rest
Anthropic (Claude API) LLM reasoning for the agent conversation content and relevant excerpts of Gmail / Calendar / Contacts data needed for the current task. OAuth tokens are never sent. zero-retention API terms.
OpenAI LLM reasoning for the agent same as above. zero-retention API terms; no training on API data.
Google (Gemini / Vertex AI) LLM reasoning for the agent same as above. processed under Google Cloud's Vertex AI terms, which prohibit training on customer data.
Formspree handling early-access interest form submissions on this website only name and email address submitted via the landing page form. no Google Workspace data.

we will update this list before adding any new subprocessor that processes user data, and notify active users of material changes.

where your data lives & how it's protected

  • all durable user data is stored on AWS in the ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) region.
  • each user's agent runs in an isolated sandbox; one user's agent cannot read another user's data.
  • data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • OAuth tokens are held in a dedicated secrets store, encrypted with keys separate from the application database, and only accessible to the secrets-aware proxy.
  • access to production infrastructure is limited to the developer (Leonardo Borges Barbosa) and requires multi-factor authentication. all administrative access is logged.
  • we follow the principle of least privilege: only the Gmail scopes listed above are requested, and each scope is tied to a specific user-facing feature.

human access to your data

in normal operation, no human at liv4all reads your Gmail content, calendar entries, or conversation history. the only circumstances in which a human may view your data are:

  • with your explicit consent — for example, when you share a debug bundle while reporting an issue, or ask us to investigate why liv handled a specific thread a particular way.
  • security & abuse — narrow, time-bound access to investigate a credible security incident or abuse of the service.
  • legal compliance — when required by a valid legal process under Australian law.

any such access is logged. we do not have a support team casually browsing user data, and we do not permit our subprocessors to do so either.

data retention

  • conversation history & workspace files — retained for as long as your account is active.
  • cached Gmail content — purged automatically within 24 hours of being fetched.
  • OAuth tokens — retained while the connection is active; deleted immediately when you disconnect a service or delete your account.
  • server logs — operational and security logs are retained for up to 30 days and then automatically deleted.
  • you can request full deletion of all your data at any time by emailing support@agent.liv4all.com with the subject line "delete my account". we will acknowledge within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days, including from backups on their normal rotation.

your rights

under the Australian Privacy Principles (and, where applicable, GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access — ask what data we hold about you.
  • correct — update inaccurate information.
  • delete — request full removal of your data (see retention above).
  • revoke — disconnect any third-party service at any time via Google account permissions. liv will stop accessing that service immediately and purge any cached content within 24 hours.
  • export — request a machine-readable copy of your conversation history and workspace files.
  • complain — if you believe we have mishandled your data, you can contact us directly or lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

children

liv is not intended for use by anyone under 18. we do not knowingly collect data from minors. if you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete the account.

changes to this policy

we may update this policy as the product evolves. we'll notify active users of material changes — especially any change to the set of scopes we request or the subprocessors we use — before those changes take effect. continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

contact

questions about your data or this policy? reach out at support@agent.liv4all.com. for data-subject requests (access, correction, deletion, export), use the same address with the relevant subject line.
