frequently asked questions

everything you want to know before handing an AI your calendar and inbox.

// the basics
what problem does liv4all solve — and why now?

most people spend hours each week on low-value admin: scheduling meetings, chasing emails, managing their calendar. AI is finally good enough to handle all of that autonomously — not just answer questions, but actually do things. liv4all puts that capability in your hands.

who is liv4all built for?

anyone who wants their time back. if you've ever wished you had an assistant handling the small stuff so you could focus on what actually matters — that's who this is for.

how is this different from chatgpt or siri?

chatgpt answers questions. siri sets timers. liv4all takes action — it connects to your actual accounts, operates on your behalf, and gets things done without you needing to babysit it. the difference is agency.

// capabilities
what can liv4all do?

during alpha, liv connects to your google account and handles:

  • alpha email — reads, drafts, and sends on your behalf
  • alpha calendar — schedules meetings, creates invites, manages your time
  • alpha contacts — finds people, surfaces context when you need it
  • alpha web browsing — fill forms, make bookings, automate web tasks
  • alpha proactive briefings — daily summaries, meeting reminders, follow-up nudges

liv also gets their own identity: a dedicated email address and telegram profile, so they can reach you — and others — directly. no new apps to learn. they meet you where you already are.

post-alpha, liv goes further:

  • coming soon phone calls — books restaurants, appointments, and more via real PSTN calls
  • coming soon WhatsApp — same liv, on the platform you already use most
  • coming soon Slack — manage your day without leaving your workspace
does liv have their own identity?

yes. each liv agent gets a dedicated email address and telegram profile. this means they can reach out to people on your behalf — scheduling with contacts, following up on threads — without needing to borrow your accounts.

they meet people where they already are. no new apps required.

// privacy & data
is my data isolated from other users?

yes — completely. every liv agent runs in its own isolated environment. your conversations, files, calendar data, and emails are completely separated from every other user's — by design, not just by policy. there are multiple layers of technical isolation ensuring one user can never access another's data.

is my data encrypted?

your data is encrypted both when it's stored (at rest) and when it's moving between systems (in transit). we use modern encryption standards across our entire infrastructure — databases, file storage, and all network connections.

where does liv run?

liv runs on AWS infrastructure in private networks that aren't directly reachable from the internet. only the specific connections needed to talk to telegram (or your email) are allowed through. we use the same cloud security building blocks that banks and healthcare companies rely on.

what does liv4all have access to?

each agent can only access your data — your emails, your calendar, your files. even internally, permissions are scoped so that each agent only has access to the specific resources it needs and nothing more.

liv4all connects to your google account via official OAuth — the same standard your other apps use. you control what you grant access to, and you can revoke it any time from your google account settings.

is my data used to train AI models?

no. your conversations and files are never used to train AI models — not ours, not anyone else's. they exist solely to power your assistant.

will liv send emails without my permission?

no. liv will never send an email on your behalf without showing you a draft first and getting your explicit confirmation. no surprises.

can i delete my data?

you're in control. you can request to access, export, or delete your data at any time. if you've connected services like google calendar, you can revoke access through those services whenever you want. full data deletion is completed within 30 days of a request.

see our privacy policy for the full picture.
