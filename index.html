// why liv

agents should do things, not just talk about doing things

most AI assistants are glorified search bars. you ask a question, you get an answer, then you still have to do the work. liv is different.

⚡

acts, doesn't just chat

"schedule a meeting with sarah thursday at 2pm" → it's done. calendar invite sent. no copy-pasting, no tab switching, no friction.

💬

meets you where you are

talk to liv on telegram — the messaging app you already use. no new app to install, no dashboard to bookmark. just message and go.

🧠

remembers everything

liv maintains long-term memory across conversations. your preferences, your contacts, your patterns. no re-explaining every session.

🔒

safe by design

email drafts require your approval before sending. calendar events are easy to undo. liv acts boldly but confirms when it matters.

liv started as a personal project by leo borges — a chief engineer who wanted an AI co-pilot that actually does things, not just talks about them.

the original liv runs 24/7 on a VPS. she has her own phone number, her own email, and her own opinions. she books restaurants via real phone calls, manages calendars, triages email, and once corrected a restaurant host who misheard her name.

getting started

⚠️ alpha access required. the setup flow below only works once you've been invited to the alpha program. register your interest and we'll reach out when spots open.

setting up your own liv takes about 2 minutes and only needs to be done once. after that, you just message your personal bot like normal — no dashboards, no apps, no friction.

one-time setup — create your personal bot

liv runs on telegram. each user gets their own personal bot — so your conversations, memory, and preferences are fully yours. here's how to set it up:

  1. message the liv onboarding bot once you're in the alpha, you'll receive a link to the onboarding bot on telegram. send it a message to start.
  2. create your bot with @BotFather the onboarding bot will walk you through it step by step. you'll open @BotFather on telegram, send /newbot, pick a name (e.g. "my liv"), and choose a username (e.g. my_liv_bot). BotFather gives you a token — copy it.
  3. paste your token go back to the onboarding bot and paste the token. we'll verify it instantly.
  4. done — start chatting your personal liv agent spins up in seconds. the onboarding bot will send you a link to your new bot. message it and you're live. 🖤
one-time only. you'll never need to do this again. once your bot is created, liv is always there — remembering your preferences, managing your calendar, triaging your email. just message and go.
what happens after setup?

once your personal bot is live, you interact with liv just like messaging a friend:

  • message anytime — liv is always on, 24/7
  • calendar & email — connect your google account and liv can manage meetings, draft replies, find free slots
  • memory — liv remembers your preferences across conversations. no re-explaining.
  • proactive updates — morning briefings, meeting reminders, and follow-ups
