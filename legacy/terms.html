connecting Google Workspace

liv connects to Google Workspace on your behalf. every scope is shown to you on Google's consent screen before access is granted. the scopes liv requests are:

  • Google Calendar — to check availability, create and update events, and manage invites.
  • Google Contacts (read-only) — to find the right person when you ask liv to schedule or reach out to someone.
  • Gmail — modify (gmail.modify) — the single Gmail permission liv requests. it lets liv read and summarise your inbox, draft replies in your drafts folder, send drafted messages only after you explicitly confirm, create and manage labels, archive handled threads, and mark messages as read during inbox triage.

liv will never permanently delete email from your account. the gmail.modify scope explicitly does not grant permanent-delete capability. any message liv moves is recoverable by you from your Gmail Trash or All Mail folder.

liv will never use your Gmail data for advertising, credit scoring, lending decisions, or any financial-eligibility determination. these are hard red lines described in more detail in our privacy policy.

every scope above is optional. Google's consent screen lets you uncheck any scope you don't want to grant before approving the connection. liv is designed to work with whatever subset you grant — features that depend on a scope you declined will simply be unavailable. you can add or remove scopes later by re-running the connection, and revoke everything at once from your Google account permissions page.

your use of Google Workspace through liv is also governed by Google's Terms of Service. you can revoke liv's access at any time via Google account permissions; liv will stop accessing Gmail, Calendar, and Contacts immediately, and any cached Gmail content is purged within 24 hours.

liv4all's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
