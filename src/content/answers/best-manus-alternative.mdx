"Best" only means something once you know what you are optimising for. Manus is a polished cloud-sandbox autonomous agent, acquired by Meta in December 2025, but it is solo, invite-gated, credit-priced, and has no integrations. People leave it for different reasons: some want open-source control, some want it plugged into hundreds of apps, some just want a standing assistant in their inbox without the maintenance.

So rather than crown one winner, match the alternative to the reason you are looking.

## How it works

The strong alternatives fall into three camps.

Open-source control: [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) is a free, MIT-licensed agent you self-host. You get a persistent [agent loop](/answers/how-an-ai-agent-loop-works), full ownership, and direct OAuth integrations, in exchange for roughly 1–3 hours a month of ops.

Broad integrations: Lindy runs around $49.99/month and advertises 4000+ integrations, aimed at workflow automation across many SaaS apps. See our [Lindy alternative](/answers/lindy-ai-alternative) page for the trade-offs.

Managed personal agent: Liv is OpenClaw's agent loop and real Gmail/Calendar/Telegram integrations wrapped in managed infrastructure, with CASA Tier 2 security and encrypted vaults, at flat pricing.

## Worked example

| If you want… | Best fit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full control, open-source, lowest cash cost | Self-hosted OpenClaw | MIT licence, infra under $100/mo, you own it |
| Many SaaS workflow integrations | Lindy | ~$49.99/mo, 4000+ integrations |
| A standing email/calendar assistant, no ops | Liv | Managed, CASA Tier 2, Pro $79/Max $149 |
| One-off autonomous sandbox tasks | Manus itself | Strong at isolated, self-contained jobs |

If the thing you missed in Manus was real account access without weekend maintenance, the managed OpenClaw route is the most direct swap.

## Common questions

### Is there a free Manus alternative?
Yes. [OpenClaw](/answers/what-is-openclaw) is free and open-source; you pay only for hosting and LLM usage. [AutoGPT](/answers/autogpt-alternative) is another free, local option.

### What's the closest managed alternative?
Liv, because it runs the same OpenClaw agent loop but hosts it for you with built-in integrations and security.

### Does Lindy do email triage like Manus?
Lindy focuses on multi-app workflow automation rather than a persistent personal assistant. The two solve different problems.

### Which is cheapest overall?
Self-hosted OpenClaw, on cash cost. Factor in your time at 1–3 hours a month and the [build-vs-buy maths](/answers/build-vs-buy-ai-agent) shifts.

### Can I migrate my Manus tasks directly?
No tool offers a one-click import, since Manus has no exportable integrations. You set up workflows fresh in whichever alternative you choose.

### Is Liv affiliated with Manus?
No. Liv is an independent managed service built on OpenClaw, not affiliated with or endorsed by Manus.
