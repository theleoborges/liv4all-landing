Maybe some of you saw this coming….

A few weeks ago [I wrote about Liv](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7431820385861419009/), my personal AI assistant that manages my calendar, handles my emails, updates my website, books restaurants and does all sorts of web automation tasks.

I added it to a few group chats and friends kept asking "can I get one?" — well, that question turned into a side project called Liv4All — the very site you're visiting!

It's very early. I opened a small alpha phase this past weekend with a handful of users.

The idea is simple: take what made Liv useful (i.e.: a PA that actually does things, bounded by real security guardrails), and make it accessible to people who just want to get things done.

I'm keeping it intentionally small to learn what's working and what needs to get better.

If you're curious and want early access, dm me.

P.S.: Two independent users who don't know each other and live in different countries both specified "world domination" when asked what they'd be using Liv4All for. Do with that what you will.
