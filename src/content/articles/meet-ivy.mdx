Quick one. Ivy is live 👋

Ivy is Liv's sidekick: a Q&A agent that sits on your website and answers your visitors' questions as they browse. She speaks in the brand voice you define, and draws from whatever knowledge you point her at: your public pages, internal documents, or both.

She's already running on this very site. Look for her in the corner and ask about Liv, pricing, anything you're curious about.
