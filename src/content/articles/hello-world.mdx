this is the articles section. we'll use it to share notes on building liv —
decisions we've made, things we've learned, and the occasional strong opinion
on where personal AI is heading.

## why an articles section?

most of what we ship never gets explained. a changelog tells you *what*
changed. an article tells you *why*. that "why" is the thing we want to
share — because the interesting parts of building an AI agent aren't the
features; they're the tradeoffs.

## what to expect

- **product notes.** how and why we made specific design calls.
- **technical posts.** the infrastructure, the safeguards, the failure modes.
- **field reports.** what happens when real people use an always-on AI agent.

no schedule. we write when we have something worth saying.
