Back in early March I launched Liv4All, a personal AI agent platform for people who actually want one in their corner, 24/7. No demo and no waitlist theatre. Real usage. From day one.

My target: 100 alpha users, a number Google chose for me. Google caps OAuth apps at exactly 100 until you clear security verification, and OAuth isn't optional when you're doing email + calendar.

We're here now, 100 signups. Real people using it in production today.

Which means sign-ups are closed while I go through the wringer. Want in when they reopen? Hit the [waitlist](/#signup).

P.S. I'm only mildly annoyed at Google. CASA Tier 2 takes weeks and isn't fun, but the reason's legit: they want proof you're handling user data properly. Which, yeah, same energy.

P.P.S. oh, the video? my friend Cheryl fed screenshots of Liv's messages into CapCut and had it generate an emo song about them. 🤣 Legit the best marketing collateral, and I had nothing to do with it.
